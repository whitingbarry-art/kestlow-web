Add the privacy notice, terms of service and refund policy (KES-37), open to anyone at `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/refunds` and linked from the sign-up form, from the desktop sidebar (with About us and Contact us, each with its icon) and, on phones and the public pages, from the footer. The privacy notice says, in plain English, what Kestlow actually keeps and why, the legal basis for each, who processes it, how long it's kept and how to use your rights, with the ICO registration number to follow. The refund policy has the 30-day money-back guarantee on a first payment. The words live in `data/legal.ts`, so they can be changed without touching the pages.
