Add sign-in, register and sign-out (KES-23). Signed-out people now see a "Sign in to Kestlow" screen instead of the app, with links to create an account and to get help with a forgotten password (resets arrive with KES-41). Registering asks for a name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, with a Weak, Medium or Strong meter and a tip as they type. Only passwords that reach Strong are accepted, here and by the API, and the hint says so. Strength is judged with zxcvbn, the way attackers guess, counting their own name and email as easy to guess, rather than with capital, number and symbol rules (NCSC advice). The checker's dictionaries load only when someone starts creating an account. Better Auth's errors are shown in plain words, and switching between the screens moves focus to the new heading. Once signed in, the app appears and stays signed in across reloads; "Sign out" in the sidebar and the mobile top bar ends the session and clears that user's cached leads. The sidebar shows who's signed in above Sign out, and the waitlist page has a "Sign in" link for people who already have an account. Leads requests now send the session cookie, and text inputs share one style across forms.
