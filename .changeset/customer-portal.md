Manage a plan in Stripe's Customer Portal (KES-51). The Account page and the pricing page now have a "Manage my plan" button for anyone who has paid for a plan, opening the portal to change or cancel it, update the card or download invoices, instead of asking them to email billing@. If the portal can't open, it says why, or offers billing@ as before.

The Terms no longer offer pausing a plan (Barry, 2 November): a plan is cancelled instead, and founding members keep their price for as long as they stay subscribed.

The Terms now match the Stripe settings chosen on 2 November: the trial reminder comes "at least 3 days" before the trial ends (Stripe sends it 7 days before), and a failed payment keeps the plan while Stripe tries again, then cancels it if the payment still can't be taken, rather than a fixed 7 days.
