Modern scroll bars: slim, rounded and amber, a softer shade of the logo's that turns full amber on hover, in light and dark mode, without Windows' grey track and arrow buttons. The trade filter buttons always stay on one line, with no bar at all, on a computer and a phone alike: an amber triangle shows on a soft fade at whichever end has more, outlined while the row is still and filled while it moves or is hovered, and glides the row along (`ScrollRow`, reusable for any sideways row).
