Make the app usable with screen readers, keyboards, low vision and reduced-motion settings, and test it. Every story is now checked with axe against WCAG 2.2 AA, and theme colours are contrast-tested in light and dark. Fixes include: the mobile lead details panel is hidden from keyboards and screen readers when closed, takes focus when opened, closes on Escape and returns focus; stronger contrast for match tags, the switch's off state and the selected-lead border; a visible focus ring; a skip link; headings for leads and settings sections; per-view page titles; a labelled distance slider; decorative icons hidden from screen readers; and the theme following the device's dark-mode setting.
