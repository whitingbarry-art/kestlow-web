Tidy-ups from the self-reviews (KES-120). While a new search loads, the lead count waits for the new number instead of briefly showing the last search's. On an iPhone or iPad, the steps for adding Kestlow to the Home Screen show calmly as help, in a soft box, rather than in red like an error; real problems stay red.

The line under "New leads near you" now gives the total as well as the new ones: "2 new today, of 3,456 leads within 10 miles", or "3,456 leads within 10 miles" when none are new today, and "No leads within 10 miles yet" when there are none. The count under the search box shows only while searching, so it doesn't repeat the heading.
